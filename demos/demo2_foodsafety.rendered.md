# Sell-or-Hold: Batch Safety Decision Workshop

Design Curriculum Document — UPCC v2.0 spine

---

## 1. Course Identity & Claim

**Title:** Sell-or-Hold: Batch Safety Decision Workshop

**Claim** — what this course commits to producing in the learner:
> Vendors can decide, unassisted, whether today's fermented-sauce batch is safe to sell using a pH reading against a published threshold.

**Non-claims** — explicitly out of scope, stated so no one over-reads the claim above:
> Does not certify food-handling licensure, does not cover recall procedures, does not replace regulatory inspection.

---

## 2. Live Problem & Purpose

- **Situation:** Street-food vendors currently decide batch safety by taste and smell, which has caused at least two spoilage incidents this quarter.
- **Purpose of this course:** Vendor faces the same daily decision but now backs it with a numeric reading instead of gut feel.

---

## 3. Learner Profile & Barriers

- **Prior knowledge assumed:** Vendors have run their stalls for years; no prior exposure to pH meters or numeric thresholds.
- **Barriers:** Low literacy with technical instruments; workshop must be entirely hands-on, spoken, in-person — no reading-heavy materials.

---

## 4. Framing — Working Words

One block per working word (the term/lens that reframes the problem into an actionable question):


- **Term:** safe-range reading, not gut-feel taste-test
  - Opens: what does 'safe enough' actually mean numerically?
  - Changed questions: what threshold am I comparing this reading to?


---

## 5. Route Map (question → domain → tool → workflow → criteria → skill)

| Question | Domain | Tool / function | Workflow | Criteria | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Is this batch inside the published safe pH range for this product category? | food_safety | pH_meter_reading | measure -> compare_to_threshold_card -> log_batch -> decide_sell_or_hold | reading falls inside the published safe range printed on the threshold card | basic_meter_use_and_threshold_lookup |


---

## 6. Capability Outcomes (incl. Human Return)

- **Overall capability claim:** Vendor can take a pH reading and correctly classify a batch as sell or hold.
- **Human Return** (what the learner can do unassisted — the proof the capability is theirs):
  Given a real batch sample with no coach present, vendor takes the reading, checks it against the threshold card, and states sell or hold correctly.

---

## 7. Content Architecture

- **Must know:** Where the safe range is on the threshold card for their product.
- **Must do:** Physically take a pH reading with the meter on a real sample.

---
