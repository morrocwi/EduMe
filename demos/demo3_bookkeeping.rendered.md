# Weekly Bookkeeping Habit for Micro-Business Owners

Design Curriculum Document — UPCC v2.0 spine

---

## 1. Course Identity & Claim

**Title:** Weekly Bookkeeping Habit for Micro-Business Owners

**Claim** — what this course commits to producing in the learner:
> Owner can close out a week's cash-in/cash-out ledger in under 20 minutes without an accountant.

**Non-claims** — explicitly out of scope, stated so no one over-reads the claim above:
> Does not cover tax filing, payroll, or formal audited statements.

---

## 2. Live Problem & Purpose

- **Situation:** Owners track sales on scraps of paper or memory and discover cash shortfalls months later with no way to trace them.
- **Purpose of this course:** Owner closes a simple weekly ledger routinely enough that a shortfall is caught within the week it happens.

---

## 3. Learner Profile & Barriers

- **Prior knowledge assumed:** Comfortable with a smartphone; has never used a ledger app or spreadsheet for the business.
- **Barriers:** No spare weekday time; must be fully async, self-paced, spread over several weeks to become a habit, not a one-off lesson.

## 6. Capability Outcomes (incl. Human Return)

- **Overall capability claim:** Owner can close a weekly cash ledger and spot a shortfall the same week it occurs.
- **Human Return** (what the learner can do unassisted — the proof the capability is theirs):
  Given a week of real transactions, owner enters them and reconciles cash-on-hand within 20 minutes without help.

---

## 7. Content Architecture

- **Must know:** The three ledger fields (in, out, running balance) and where cash-on-hand should match.
- **Must do:** Actually enter one real week of transactions and reconcile.
