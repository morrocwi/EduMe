# Reading a Seed Packet: Live Online Workshop for Community Gardeners

Design Curriculum Document — UPCC v2.0 spine

---

## 1. Course Identity & Claim

**Title:** Reading a Seed Packet: Live Online Workshop for Community Gardeners

**Claim** — what this course commits to producing in the learner:
> Participants can choose which seeds to sow this month for their own plot by reading a seed packet and a local planting calendar.

**Non-claims** — explicitly out of scope, stated so no one over-reads the claim above:
> Does not certify horticultural expertise, soil testing, pest diagnosis or commercial growing.

---

## 2. Live Problem & Purpose

- **Situation:** New community gardeners sow seeds at the wrong time or depth because they do not know how to read the information on a seed packet, and then lose a season.
- **Purpose of this course:** A gardener can look at any seed packet and a planting calendar and decide, with reasons, whether to sow now, wait, or choose another seed.

---

## 3. Learner Profile & Barriers

- **Prior knowledge assumed:** Has a plot or a balcony container and has bought seeds before; has never used a planting calendar.
- **Barriers:** Works in the evenings and can only attend by videoconference from home; some have small screens and unstable connections; some feel shy about asking questions in front of a group.

---

## 4. Framing — Working Words

One block per working word (the term/lens that reframes the problem into an actionable question):


- **Term:** sow-now test
  - Opens: a packet is a set of conditions to compare with today, not a recipe to follow blindly
  - Changed questions: do today's conditions match what this packet asks for?

---

## 6. Capability Outcomes (incl. Human Return)

- **Overall capability claim:** Gardener can decide, with a stated reason, whether to sow a given seed now.
- **Human Return** (what the learner can do unassisted — the proof the capability is theirs):
  Some weeks later, shown a packet not seen in the workshop and a calendar, the gardener decides sow, wait or swap and gives the reason, without help from the instructor, a peer or an AI tool.

---

## 7. Content Architecture

- **Must know:** The parts of a seed packet (sowing window, depth, spacing, days to emerge) and how a planting calendar is read.
- **Must do:** Compare at least two real packets from their own collection against the calendar and record a decision for each.
