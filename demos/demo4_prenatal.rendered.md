# Community Health Volunteer Prenatal Checkup Program

Design Curriculum Document — UPCC v2.0 spine

---

## 1. Course Identity & Claim

**Title:** Community Health Volunteer Prenatal Checkup Program

**Claim** — what this course commits to producing in the learner:
> Volunteer can independently conduct a standard prenatal home-visit checkup and correctly flag when a case needs referral to a clinic.

**Non-claims** — explicitly out of scope, stated so no one over-reads the claim above:
> Does not authorize the volunteer to diagnose, prescribe, or treat any complication directly.

---

## 2. Live Problem & Purpose

- **Situation:** Rural households often skip prenatal checkups until a complication is already advanced, because the nearest clinic requires a half-day trip.
- **Purpose of this course:** A volunteer can catch warning signs early, at the household, before a trip to the clinic becomes urgent.

---

## 3. Learner Profile & Barriers

- **Prior knowledge assumed:** Volunteers are literate community members with no formal medical training; some have informal caregiving experience.
- **Barriers:** Cannot travel to a central training site often; program must run mostly in-field, over several weeks, with a supervising nurse present periodically.

---

## 4. Framing — Working Words

One block per working word (the term/lens that reframes the problem into an actionable question):


- **Term:** checklist-against-threshold, not how-she-looks
  - Opens: a checkup is a fixed checklist against a threshold, not a judgment call about how the mother 'looks'
  - Changed questions: does this specific reading cross the referral threshold?

---

## 6. Capability Outcomes (incl. Human Return)

- **Overall capability claim:** Volunteer can complete a full prenatal home-visit checklist and correctly decide refer-or-continue.
- **Human Return** (what the learner can do unassisted — the proof the capability is theirs):
  With the supervising nurse absent, volunteer completes a real household visit and makes a correct refer-or-continue call against the published thresholds.

---

## 7. Content Architecture

- **Must know:** Every threshold on the referral checklist and what crossing it means.
- **Must do:** Perform a full real household visit and complete the checklist.

---
